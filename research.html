Research

My work sits where signal processing meets clinical diagnosis. The common thread is turning a raw physiological recording into a small set of features that a classifier can use reliably, and then testing that pipeline honestly.

EEG-based detection of neurological and psychiatric disorders

Feature extraction and classification pipelines for Alzheimer's disease, mild cognitive impairment, schizophrenia and ADHD from scalp EEG. Recent work puts weight on subject-wise and multi-cohort evaluation so that reported accuracy reflects performance on unseen people, and releases code so results can be reproduced.

Fourier-Bessel and empirical wavelet decomposition of cardiac and muscle signals

Adaptive decomposition methods (FBSE-EWT and its multivariate extension MVFBSE-EWT) that split ECG, vectorcardiogram and EMG signals into meaningful sub-bands before feature extraction. Applied to posterior myocardial infarction, bundle branch block, valvular heart disease and eye movement classification.

Arrhythmia detection for mobile healthcare

Event-driven and multirate processing combined with variational mode and wavelet decomposition and metaheuristic optimization, aimed at low-cost arrhythmia classification on wearable and mobile devices. Joint work with Dr. Saeed Mian Qaisar.

Respiratory and heart sound analysis

Current directions
