Research Themes

What I work on

The common thread is turning a raw physiological recording into features a classifier can use reliably, and then testing that pipeline honestly on unseen subjects.

EEG-based neurological and psychiatric disorders

Alzheimer's disease, MCI, schizophrenia and ADHD from scalp EEG, with subject-wise and multi-cohort evaluation and released code.

Fourier-Bessel and empirical wavelet decomposition

FBSE-EWT and multivariate MVFBSE-EWT for myocardial infarction, bundle branch block, valvular disease and eye movement EMG.

Arrhythmia detection for mobile healthcare

Multirate and event-driven processing with VMD, wavelets and metaheuristic optimisation for low-cost wearable ECG analysis.

Respiratory and heart sound analysis

Electronic stethoscope recordings analysed with EMD, Stockwell transform and MFCC features for early detection in children and adults.

Medical image analysis

Collaborative work on glaucoma detection from fundus images and graph-attention networks for brain tumour identification.

Current direction

New multichannel decomposition methods for physiological signals and leakage-free benchmarks across public datasets.

Academic background

Profile & Education

I received my PhD from Sant Gadge Baba Amravati University in 2018 for work on signal processing of human body sounds for early disease detection in telemedicine, under Dr. Vasif Ahmed. Research training at IIT Indore with Prof. Ram Bilas Pachori in 2020 started the Fourier-Bessel and empirical wavelet line of work that appeared in IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence, IEEE Transactions on Human-Machine Systems, Expert Systems with Applications and Biomedical Signal Processing and Control.

At SNIST I lead the institute research office as Associate Dean (Research), covering research policy, the seed grant scheme and publication norms, and I co-guide a PhD scholar.
