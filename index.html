About

I work on biomedical signal processing. My research develops signal decomposition and machine learning methods that detect disease from physiological signals such as EEG, ECG, vectorcardiogram, EMG, and heart and lung sounds. A large part of my recent work is EEG-based detection of neurological and psychiatric disorders, including Alzheimer's disease, mild cognitive impairment, schizophrenia and ADHD, together with evaluation protocols that keep data from the same subject out of both the training and the test sets.

My earlier work, with Prof. Ram Bilas Pachori (IIT Indore), introduced Fourier-Bessel series expansion based empirical wavelet transform (FBSE-EWT) pipelines for myocardial infarction, bundle branch block, valvular heart disease and eye movement classification. This work appeared in IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence, IEEE Transactions on Human-Machine Systems, Expert Systems with Applications, Biomedical Signal Processing and Control and Digital Signal Processing. I received my PhD from Sant Gadge Baba Amravati University in 2018 for work on processing body sounds for early disease detection in telemedicine.

I am Associate Editor of Traitement du Signal and Academic Editor of PLOS Digital Health, a Fellow of IETE and a Member of IEEE. Besides university teaching, I design and deliver hands-on training programmes for industry in computer vision, 4G/5G radio access networks and fiber optics, including onsite delivery in Muscat, Oman.
